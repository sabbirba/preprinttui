# Code of Conduct

We are committed to keeping `preprinttui` and the PreConnect ecosystem a friendly, safe, and welcoming project for students, contributors, and maintainers.

## Our Standards

We expect everyone to:

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Focus on the problem, not the person.
- Help make the project easier for new contributors to understand and join.
- Respect privacy, consent, and safety when discussing student data or screenshots.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or threats
- Publishing private information, tokens, or passwords
- Repeated disruption or trolling

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through the private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
